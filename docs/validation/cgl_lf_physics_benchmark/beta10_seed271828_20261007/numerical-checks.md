A [frozen-header GPU probe](/lustre/orion/ast207/proj-shared/dfielding/CGL/WO2/physics-benchmark/analysis-checks/restart-gpu-probe/README.md) reproduced exactly those three cells. Collision encoding used `0.5*sum(momentum²)/rho`; canonical C2P used `0.5*(1/rho)*sum(momentum²)`. Their enclosing internal-energy expressions differed by 1–2 ULPs on the GPU, even where separately stored kinetic energies agreed. Cached collision pressures were admissible, but independent C2P of conserved E/A yielded wall margins −8.88e−16, −6.66e−16 and −8.88e−16. Supplying canonical internal energy to the unchanged wall helper eliminated all three failures; [exact states, outputs and hashes](/lustre/orion/ast207/proj-shared/dfielding/CGL/WO2/physics-benchmark/analysis-checks/restart-gpu-probe/summary.json) are retained.

Numerical fix `71ad25ebce73d33db048defd8f585a7dce0528c2` changes only `src/eos/cgl_mhd.cpp`: active collision encoding uses canonical reciprocal arithmetic, then always recovers modified pressures from the current conserved energy and returned A. The conserved-A formulation and existing physical wall projection remain in place; only the intended wall/collision operation changes A. Density, momentum, total energy and B remain unchanged by that operation. No thresholds, tolerances, strict checks, startup repairs or checkpoint bytes were changed. The old run was preserved and the corrected canonical realization restarted from t=0.
