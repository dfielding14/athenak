\subsection{Energetic-Particle State and Equation of Motion}

The paper-mode particle state stores mass-normalized momentum
$\boldsymbol{w}\equiv\boldsymbol{p}/m$. The derived Lorentz factor, velocity,
and kinetic energy per unit mass are
\begin{align}
\gamma &=
\left(1+\frac{|\boldsymbol{w}|^2}{\mathbb{C}^2}\right)^{1/2},
\label{eq:gamma}\\
\boldsymbol{v} &= \frac{\boldsymbol{w}}{\gamma},
\label{eq:velocity}\\
\epsilon_{\rm kin} &= (\gamma-1)\mathbb{C}^2.
\label{eq:kinetic-energy}
\end{align}
Here $\mathbb{C}$ is the configured artificial cosmic-ray light speed.

\subsection{Deposited-Current Normalization}

For a uniform streaming carrier, the exact AthenaK deposited-current closure is
\begin{equation}
\left(\frac{J_{\rm CR}}{c}\right)_{\rm dep}
=
\frac{{\rm PPC}\,q_{\rm scale}\,[q/(mc)]\,v_{\rm CR}}
     {V_{\rm root\,cell}}
=2B_gk_0,
\label{eq:bell-deposited-current}
\end{equation}
where ${\rm PPC}$ is particles per root cell, $q_{\rm scale}$ is
\texttt{deposit\_qscale}, and $V_{\rm root\,cell}$ is computed from the global
root-mesh extents and counts. A fixed ${\rm PPC}\,q_{\rm scale}$ therefore
changes the physical current when dimension or root resolution changes unless
the volume dependence is included. Changing only meshblock/rank decomposition
must leave Equation~\ref{eq:bell-deposited-current} invariant.

The historical Bell preparation omitted $V_{\rm root\,cell}$ from its closure
and targeted $2B_g\mathbb{C}k_0$ rather than $2B_gk_0$. Both errors change the
physical Bell problem and invalidate every inherited historical Bell artifact
for qualification.
