\subsection{Energetic-Particle State and Equation of Motion}

The paper-mode particle state stores mass-normalized momentum
$\boldsymbol{w}\equiv\boldsymbol{p}/m$. The derived Lorentz factor, velocity,
and kinetic energy per unit mass are
\begin{align}
\gamma &=
\left(1+\frac{|\boldsymbol{w}|^2}{\mathbb{C}^2}\right)^{1/2},
\label{eq:gamma}\\
\boldsymbol{v} &= \frac{\boldsymbol{w}}{\gamma},
\label{eq:velocity}\\
\epsilon_{\rm kin} &= (\gamma-1)\mathbb{C}^2.
\label{eq:kinetic-energy}
\end{align}
Here $\mathbb{C}$ is the configured artificial cosmic-ray light speed.

\begin{enumerate}
\item Deposit initial-position particle moments required by the gas predictor.
\item Advance particle positions to the midpoint.
\item Advance the MHD predictor and apply documented particle source terms.
\item Interpolate midpoint gas velocity and magnetic field to particles.

relativistic Boris update.
\item Record particle momentum and kinetic-energy changes.
\item Advance particle positions to the final state.
\item Deposit recorded changes and apply the opposite gas update.
\item Apply physical boundaries, migration, synchronization, and constrained
transport in the documented task order.
\end{enumerate}
